About Smart Reminder Pro

Smart Reminder Pro is a privacy-focused browser utility designed to help users stay on track with daily routines. Whether managing hydration schedules, medication intervals, or work breaks, our lightweight web tool delivers audio and visual alerts directly within your device browser.

Key Features & Daily Uses

  • Hydration Tracking: Schedule regular hourly alerts to maintain healthy water intake throughout the day.
  • Medication Management: Set precise timed alarms to ensure timely dosage of prescriptions and supplements.
  • Screen Break (20-20-20 Rule): Reduce digital eye strain by setting recurring 20-minute reminders to look away from screen monitors.

Privacy & How It Works

All schedule data and active alarms run locally inside your browser's Web Storage API. No user data, reminder titles, or timing preferences are stored on external server databases, guaranteeing privacy and offline functionality.

How to Use ReminderPro & Build Daily Habits

ReminderPro helps you stay consistent with daily routines, health check-ins, and custom deadlines right from your browser—without setting up an account. Use one-click quick routines for recurring habits or create custom reminders for specific times and dates.

Quick Add Routines Explained

  • Water (Every 1 hr): Great for steady hydration throughout study blocks or desk work without interrupting your flow.
  • Medicine (Every 8 hrs): Handy for spacing out regular tablets or supplements across a 24-hour day.
  • 20-20-20 Rule (20 mins): Helps reduce eye strain during long screen sessions by reminding you to look 20 feet away for 20 seconds every 20 minutes.
  • Walk (Every 2 hrs): Encourages brief movement breaks to refresh focus and posture during long sitting stretches.

Getting the Most Out of Custom Reminders

When adding a custom reminder, give it a clear, action-oriented title (such as "Take Vitamin D tablet" or "Submit assignment draft"), pick a category so you can scan your schedule easily, and set the exact date and time. Once saved, your upcoming tasks appear under Active Reminders so you can track what is queued next.

Tips for Reliable Browser Alerts

  1. Click "Enable Notifications": Grant notification permission in your browser prompt so alerts can pop up when a timer finishes.
  2. Keep the Browser Running: Browser-based alerts and audio chimes rely on the page or browser staying open—avoid closing the window completely when waiting on an important alert.
  3. Check Sound & Focus Settings: Make sure your device volume is up and Do Not Disturb or aggressive battery/tab-sleeping modes aren't silencing notifications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my reminders saved if I refresh the page?

Yes, reminders stored in your browser remain available across refreshes unless you clear your browser storage or site data.

Should I use Quick Add Routines or Custom Reminders?

Use Quick Add Routine for repeatable daily wellness habits on a fixed interval, and Add Custom Reminder for one-off tasks or specific calendar dates and times.

Is ReminderPro free to use?

Yes, all reminder creation tools and routine presets are free to use directly in your web browser.
